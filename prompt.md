# Carely card asset master prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: subtle background illustration for a homepage feature card
Primary request: Create a minimal, very soft editorial illustration for Carely, a calm voice companion that helps older family members by phone.
Style/medium: delicate hand-painted watercolor with sparse pencil-like contours on warm off-white paper; restrained, airy, and human; simple shapes with generous negative space
Composition/framing: portrait card composition; one small focal arrangement in the lower half; quiet open space across the upper and central areas so dark interface text remains readable
Lighting/mood: diffuse daylight; patient, reassuring, familiar, and gently optimistic
Color palette: near-white, misty sky blue, muted sage, dusty peach, and soft warm gray; extremely low contrast and lightly desaturated
Materials/textures: barely visible watercolor bloom and fine paper grain
Constraints: background artwork only; no words, letters, numbers, logos, borders, UI, people, faces, hands, medical symbols, or watermark; no harsh edges; no saturated colors; no gradients; no dramatic shadows; no 3D render; no glossy finish; no generic tech imagery; keep the full image very pale so black text can sit over it
